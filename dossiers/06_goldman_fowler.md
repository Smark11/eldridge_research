## 5. Motivation hypothesis

### Primary face (per Prince & File): **Altruist**

**Strongest evidence cluster.** Amy's documented philanthropic posture is characterized by:

1. **Low-recognition giving preference.** Her largest documented family-named institutional recognitions — Lillian Goldman Law Library at Yale ($20M, 1992); Lillian Goldman Visitors Center at Seed Savers Exchange Heritage Farm — are her *mother's* name, not her own. The Amy Goldman Fowler Fellowship at the Center for Jewish History is the rare exception, and it's a fellowship (a scholarly support mechanism), not a building.
2. **Her core advocacy (heirloom seeds, biodiversity, sustainable agriculture) is structurally an Altruist cause-frame** — the beneficiaries are anonymous future food-system participants, the timeframe is multi-generational, and the recognition tier is low. The Florens DeBevoise Medal citation describes her commitment as "purposeful philanthropy."
3. **Her academic credentials (PhD clinical psychology, Oklahoma State 1984) plus her career pivot away from the family real estate empire suggest values-driven life choices over wealth-amplification life choices.** This is structurally Altruist behavior, not Investor or Socialite behavior.
4. **The gap between her wealth ($1B+) and her named-foundation disclosure ($3.2M/yr)** is consistent with significant direct anonymous or low-recognition giving — Altruist pattern.

### Secondary face: **Dynast** (Goldman family-fortune stewardship)

**Documented but secondary.** The Goldman family-name infrastructure (Lillian Goldman Law Library, Sol Goldman Pancreatic Cancer Research Center, Lillian Goldman Visitors Center) is multigenerational and intentional. Amy participates in this Dynast structure — she serves as trustee of the Lillian Goldman Charitable Trust and uses her own Foundation, both of which extend the family-name brand. **But:** the actively-Dynastic siblings are Jane (Solil operational leader, Sol Goldman Trust controller, $10M Johns Hopkins gift driver) and to a different extent Lloyd Goldman cousin (BLDG, Northwell Pavilion).
