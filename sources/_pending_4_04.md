## Access notes / verification details

1. **WebFetch 403s encountered:** `https://www.ynhhs.org/news/gh-receives-generous-donation-in-support-of-pediatric-services` and `https://www.ynhhs.org/perspectives/gh-gift-to-honor-physician` returned HTTP 403 in this session. Content for these citations was triangulated against (a) Greenwich Sentinel direct articles, (b) Greenwich Hospital own news pages, and (c) Yale School of Medicine pages, all of which were accessible.

2. **Anonymous-donor identity:** The $25M anonymous Donor-Advised-Fund gift announced Dec 15-17, 2025 honoring Dr. Dickerman Hollister is sourced from (a) Yale School of Medicine official announcement, (b) Greenwich Hospital news, (c) Westfair, (d) Greenwich Sentinel news briefs, and (e) Patch. The donor's identity was not disclosed by the hospital or by any reporting and remains `(unknown)`.

3. **Bendheim Cancer Center original naming-gift dollar:** Not publicly disclosed in any reviewed source. The Leon Lowenstein Foundation is the institutional donor of record (Robert A. Bendheim was foundation president); the family vehicle structure and Lowenstein Foundation ProPublica filings are documented but the specific gift to Greenwich Hospital is not itemized in publicly accessible 990s reviewed.

4. **Asness/Liew giving to Greenwich Hospital:** The single direct reference is Serena Liew's support of "community health initiatives" at Greenwich Hospital, sourced from Inside Philanthropy's AQR cofounders article. No principal-tier ($1M+) gift from Cliff Asness, John Liew, David Kabiller, or their respective spouses to Greenwich Hospital is documented in any reviewed source. The Asness Family Foundation ProPublica filings do not list Greenwich Hospital as a recipient in their visible grant history.

5. **Per-area dollar allocation within the $125M Arc of Care:** Not publicly disclosed in any campaign materials reviewed (giving.greenhosp.org, Greenwich Sentinel, Greenwich Free Press, Westfair, Becker's, Patch). The hospital has disclosed only the $125M total goal and the five focus areas.

6. **Greenwich Hospital Auxiliary cumulative giving:** Reported as $792K in a single announcement; cumulative lifetime giving from the Auxiliary is likely substantially higher but not documented in a single reviewed source.

7. **Helmsley estate cumulative giving to Greenwich Hospital:** Sources report inconsistent totals — NBC Connecticut cites $9.7M; another Inside Philanthropy / Helmsley-coverage thread references "up to $23M" — likely the difference is between a single tranche ($9.7M) and cumulative giving across multiple grants. Confidence on the precise cumulative number: MEDIUM.

8. **The 1997 Bendheim Center for Finance at Princeton naming gift** ($10M from Lowenstein Foundation) is documented in Princeton's official 1997 press release and used in this case study to establish the institutional structure of the Bendheim family / Lowenstein Foundation giving vehicle, not as a Greenwich Hospital data point.

9. **Bauer family giving to Norwalk Hospital cumulative**: Sources document the cumulative giving as $50M+ across NICU naming, Emergency Care Center naming, Nursing Scholarship, and the $20M Pavilion gift. Yale Divinity School documents the Bauer family's separate $25M+ commitment to Living Village + Carol B. Bauer Hall. **No documented Bauer family giving to Greenwich Hospital found in any reviewed source.** This negative finding is itself diagnostic for Section 9.C of the case study.
