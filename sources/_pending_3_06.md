2. **[Cary Fowler — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cary_Fowler)** — retrieved 2026-05-11. 3. **[Sol Goldman — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sol_Goldman)** — retrieved 2026-05-11. Largest non-institutional NYC real estate investor 1980s, ~600 properties (one source says 1,900 commercial/residential), Chrysler Building stake holder, died Oct 1987, four children Allan/Diane/Amy/Jane, wife Lillian d. 2002, estate largest ever brought to NY Surrogate's Court.

4. **[Lloyd Goldman — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Goldman)** — retrieved 2026-05-11. **Sol Goldman's nephew = Amy's first cousin**. Founder/President of BLDG Management Co. (1984; $2B+ in real estate, 250+ properties). His father Irving was Sol's brother; sisters Katja Goldman Sonnenfeldt and Dorian Goldman Israelow. Took over BLDG with sisters in 1995 when Irving died.

5. **[Lillian Goldman Law Library — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lillian_Goldman_Law_Library)** — retrieved 2026-05-11. Yale Law School library named after $20M gift from Lillian Goldman in 1992; Sol Goldman's widow's gift. "Probably the biggest gift in the history of American legal education" per Dean Guido Calabresi. Establishes Lillian-naming precedent.
