## Known methodological gaps for this case study

1. **Gift payment structure not publicly disclosed.** Public materials describe the $10M as a commitment intended to "fast-track" the trauma initiative; whether structured as one-time cash, multi-year pledge, appreciated-asset transfer, or DAF grant is not in public domain. Given the Schwartzes' wealth profile and giving patterns, a DAF or appreciated-asset component is plausible but unconfirmed.
2. **Westfair Online article HTTP 403.** The Westfair Communications article "The Largest Single Donation in a Hospital's History" returned HTTP 403 on direct WebFetch attempts; content was triangulated through other regional press coverage of the same announcement.
3. **The Examiner News article HTTP 403.** The Examiner News local coverage similarly returned 403; trauma-care-context detail triangulated through Northwell-direct and River Journal sources.
4. **Schwartz-family private foundation identification.** No 990-PF lookup performed in this case study; if Schwartzes have a named family foundation (separate from individual DAF/personal giving), it has not been confirmed via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer in this pass. Would be a useful Phase 5 follow-up if this couple becomes a Nuvance prospect template.
5. **Gap between 2015 ($1M surgical) and 2024 ($1M behavioral health anchor) Schwartz NWH gifts.** Nine years of public silence between major NWH gifts is not explained in any source; institutional-transition (Seligman to Anderson, 2019) and Northwell-integration absorption are inferred causes but not documented.
6. **Schwartzes' relationship with NWH Foundation President Keeva Young-Wright is not directly documented** in public materials; her role as operational owner of the relationship is inferred from her position rather than confirmed by any source explicitly placing her in the November 2024 meeting.
