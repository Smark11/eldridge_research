## Context and method note

The 2025 Forbes 400 was published September 9, 2025 (44th annual edition). It now takes a $3.8B net worth to make the cut, up $500M YoY; the 400 collectively are worth a record $6.6T (source: [Forbes 400 2025 Wealthiest Americans list](https://www.newsbreak.com/trending-story/forbes-400-wealthiest-americans-list-2025-68c116a278878ebcdb5210ae); [Facebook/Forbes 44th annual list announcement](https://www.facebook.com/forbes/posts/new-york-september-9-2025-forbes-today-unveils-its-44th-annual-forbes-400-list-t/1160038932652762/), retrieved 2026-05-10).

**Scope filter applied:** This screen captured Forbes 400 (and the broader Forbes Real-Time Billionaires Index where the cut is below $3.8B) individuals whose **primary** residence is in the Nuvance service-area-relevant counties: Dutchess, Putnam, Westchester, Rockland (NY); Fairfield, Litchfield (CT). Secondary residences flagged where known. Individuals with documented NY/CT residence histories who have since moved to Florida (Tepper, Griffin, Kovner, parts of Och) are flagged but de-prioritized.

**WebFetch was denied** in this session. All claims here are grounded in WebSearch result excerpts; where claims rest on a single source, the confidence label reflects that.

Tudor Jones is in active transition (still owns Greenwich estate but seeking non-residency tax status). Stone may have shifted to Naples FL. Sinai than to Nuvance specifically.
