## Notable observations

### A. Concentration of NYP/MSK/Mt Sinai board service among this cohort

The most strategically important finding of this screen is the **dense pattern of competing-institutional ties** among Greenwich-corridor billionaires:

- **Daniel Och** — $25M Och Spine Hospital at NYP (named gift; deep NYP affiliation)
- **Doug Ostrover** — Mt Sinai Health System board AND MSK Leadership Council (dual NYC academic medical center loyalties)
- **Steven Cohen** — $50M+ across multiple NYP units; AND owns Cohen Children's Medical Center at Northwell (which is now structurally same-system as Nuvance post-merger — actually an asset, not a competing loyalty)
- **Glenn Dubin** — Dubin Breast Center at Mt Sinai Tisch (named-gift donor)
- **Igor Tulchinsky** — $5M WorldQuant Initiative at Weill Cornell (NYP-affiliated)
- **Henry Kravis** (in adjacent service area) — $110M+ MSK lifetime donor
- **Stephen Mandel** — $1.5M Greenwich Hospital (Yale-NHH, NOT a Northwell competitor; less disqualifying)
- **Cliff Asness / John Liew** — Greenwich Hospital community support (Yale-NHH)

The pattern: **transformational hedge-fund principals at the $3B+ tier in this corridor systematically route their major healthcare giving to NYC academic medical centers** (NYP, MSK, Mt Sinai, Weill Cornell). This is rationally driven by (a) personal NYC residence patterns, (b) flagship-institution prestige, (c) network effects (board cohort).

**Strategic implication for Nuvance Foundation:** The "Greenwich hedge fund as Nuvance donor base" thesis at the $25M+ transformational tier is structurally HARDER than it initially appears. The phase 0 audit verdict (centi-millionaires with deeper local affinity, not billionaires, are the structurally correct segment) is reinforced. This screen surfaced very few billionaire principals who are NOT already aligned with a competing NYC institution.

### B. Hedge fund principals with Nuvance-relevant giving patterns

Only two billionaire principals in this screen have documented **direct** Nuvance-system giving:

Repeat-donor channel. *But:* Dalio's $50M NYP Center for Health Justice (2021) is also documented, meaning Dalio's healthcare-cause giving spans both. Post-merger, this means he's structurally a **Northwell insider, not a competitor's pawn.** This is the single highest-affinity discovery in this screen — a Greenwich-corridor billionaire who is already inside the Northwell-Nuvance tent.

### C. The Bauer family bridge

The **George and Carol Bauer** ($21.5M+ cumulative to Norwalk Hospital; Bauer Family Pavilion namesake; helped raise $100M+ more from others) are explicitly Phase 1 ADVANCE-tier candidates. Their wealth tier is sub-billionaire (likely $300M-$500M range, inferred from giving capacity), but their Nuvance affinity is the strongest in the entire dataset. They are also in their 90s — life-stage signal makes succession planning a current question. The Bauer family network within Norwalk Hospital should be a Phase 2 priority for legacy-leverage strategy.

### D. The Helvey-Bauer network indicates a "donor network" pattern

The $1M Helvey gift (April 2025, honoring the Bauers' 70th wedding anniversary, designated to the Bauer Family Pavilion) reveals that Norwalk Hospital already has a coordinated donor-network around the Bauers. This network is non-hedge-fund (Dr. Helvey is a physician in California). It does suggest that **Norwalk Hospital's existing donor relationships, not the Greenwich hedge fund corridor, are the more reliable transformational-gift pipeline.**

### E. Stamford Hospital's Cohen alignment is a competitive risk for Nuvance Norwalk

Steve Cohen's $20M+ commitment to Stamford Hospital is a meaningful regional fact: Stamford Hospital is a Nuvance Norwalk Hospital DIRECT regional competitor (different system; Stamford is independent / Hartford Healthcare-aligned). The Cohens have rooted their Children's Institute brand at Stamford. **Pursuing the Cohens for a Nuvance Norwalk gift would directly cannibalize an existing Stamford Hospital relationship**, and Steve Cohen is highly unlikely to split or transfer his Fairfield-County pediatric brand.

### F. Westport-specific: surprisingly thin once Bridgewater is excluded

Despite Westport hosting Bridgewater's headquarters, no other hedge fund principals were surfaced as Westport-resident at the screened wealth tier. Dalio himself lives in Greenwich. This is consistent with the pattern that hedge fund principals concentrate their residences in Greenwich, with Westport being more of an office geography.

## Data gaps and limitations

1. **Centi-millionaire-tier principals are underrepresented.** Most $100M-$999M hedge fund partners are not Forbes-ranked and require Form ADV + direct Linked-In / SEC filing analysis to surface. The current list captures the most visible names; the long tail of Greenwich/Stamford ~$300M partners is likely much deeper than this 22-name list captures. Phase 2 should consider engaging a paid wealth-screening service or direct Form ADV review on the top 20 CT-headquartered hedge funds to surface these.
2. **Personal residence data is publicly noisy.** Several principals were confirmed to "have a property" in Greenwich or "be associated with" the area, but whether it is primary, secondary, or merely an investment property requires deeper review (property records, voter registration, etc.).
3. **DAF grants are invisible.** Several principals (Mandel via Zoom Foundation, Asness, others) explicitly route giving through DAFs. Their actual grant destinations are not public; we can only see foundation-to-DAF transfers.
4. **MSK and NYP board rosters were not fully cross-verified** for 2025-2026; some competing-loyalty flags above are inferred from documented gifts and Inside Philanthropy reporting rather than confirmed board listing. Phase 2 dossiers should confirm against current institutional websites.
5. **Spousal foundations and "next-generation" wealth** (e.g., adult children of these principals) were not screened. Several of the principals above have adult children who may themselves be approaching philanthropic life-stage.
6. **Recent 2026 net-worth volatility** is significant; figures are best-available but vary by source date.
