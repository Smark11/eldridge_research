## Scoring framework (recap — full detail in `screening_methodology.md`)

Six affinity signals, 1-5 each (total 6-30):
- **S1 Nuvance connection** — board service / patient / named gift / multi-gen legacy family
- **S2 Residential ties** — primary/secondary residence in service area
- **S3 Healthcare giving history** — past hospital gifts; named gifts $5M+ anywhere
- **S4 Cause affinity to Nuvance signature programs** — cancer, cardiac, women's health, neurosciences
- **S5 Giving vehicle sophistication** — private foundation assets, DAF activity
- **S6 Life-stage signals** — age 65+, recent liquidity event, succession behavior

**Outcome thresholds:** ADVANCE ≥ 18 | HOLD 15-17 | DROP < 15.
**Target ADVANCE pool:** 25-40. **Final short list (Phase 2):** 5-7.
