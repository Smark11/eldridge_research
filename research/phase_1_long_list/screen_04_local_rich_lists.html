Lane scope and methodology

This screen targets candidates who are well-documented in regional/local press but are not primarily surfaced by Forbes 400 or Bloomberg Billionaires Index (which are Subagents 1 and 2's lanes). Sources searched:

  • City & State New York's Hudson Valley Power 100 (2024)
  • Westfair Communications (Westchester County Business Journal, Fairfield County Business Journal, HV Biz)
  • Hartford Business Journal "Wealthiest People in CT" series
  • Westchester Magazine and Yonkers Times wealth coverage
  • Litchfield Magazine (Anne Bass / Rock Cobble Farm coverage)
  • The Lakeville Journal (Salisbury/Sharon coverage)
  • Inside Philanthropy (Greenwich/Litchfield/Westchester philanthropists)
  • Patch (regional Daily Voice / Patch coverage of local billionaires)
  • Hudson Valley Magazine "Best of" + Wealthiest features
  • ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (for foundation financial confirmation)

Wealth-estimate methodology: When a candidate's net worth is documented in a public source (Forbes, Hartford Business Journal citing Forbes, Westfair citing Forbes), I cite that figure directly. When wealth is inferred from real estate values, business holdings, or family-fortune lineage, I label that explicitly and provide the inferential basis. Per the screening methodology document, I aim for triangulation to ≥2 independent sources and acknowledge a typical 2x uncertainty band for inferred figures.

Confidence labels are applied per the methodology: (known) = documented and triangulated; (inferred) = derived from circumstantial evidence with stated reasoning; (unknown) = open question.

Hudson Valley grandee corridor — what we found

Phase 0 flagged the possibility that "descendants of Astor, Roosevelt, Delano, Aldrich, Mortimer, Roosevelt-DuPont lines" might surface as candidates. Findings:

Astor descendants (living)

  • Brooke Astor died 2007. Her son Anthony Marshall died imprisoned. Her grandsons are Philip Cryan Marshall (born 1953, professor at Roger Williams; now full-time elder-justice advocate at Beyond Brooke nonprofit) and Alexander R. Marshall (born 1953, photographer). Philip notably testified AGAINST his father in the elder-abuse case. Both grandsons received only modest inheritances from the contested estate settlement (2012). [Sources: Wikipedia/Anthony Dryden Marshall; Air Mail on the Brooke Astor case — retrieved 2026-05-10.]
  • Strategic verdict: The Marshall grandsons are unlikely to be at $300M+ wealth or to have giving capacity at Nuvance-relevant scale. DROP.
  • The remaining Astor name is institutional — Astor Foundation, etc. — and is largely directed by trustees rather than family.

Roosevelt descendants (living)

  • The Roosevelt family is large but highly diffused across generations. Living FDR descendants include Anna Eleanor Roosevelt (FDR granddaughter, age ~80s) and other grand- and great-grandchildren. None documented at $300M+ individual wealth tier; family wealth was historically modest by Gilded Age standards and has been diluted across many heirs.
  • Strategic verdict: Roosevelt-family individuals are unlikely Phase 1 candidates at the wealth tier required. DROP unless future research surfaces a specific living individual meeting the threshold.

Delano descendants

  • Largely overlap with Roosevelt branch (FDR's mother Sara Delano Roosevelt). Same diffusion problem. DROP absent specific surfaceable individual.

Aldrich descendants (living)

  • Found: Richard "Ricky" Aldrich, John Winthrop "Wint" Aldrich, Rosalind Aldrich Michahelles, plus Marina Michahelles and Sophia Michahelles still live at Rokeby in Barrytown (Dutchess County). They are descendants of Margaret Livingston Chanler Aldrich (1870-1963), who married Richard Aldrich, music critic for the NYT.
  • Critical caveat: Alexandra Aldrich's 2013 memoir "The Astor Orphan" frames the family explicitly as "Astor orphans" — descendants of Astor money who did not inherit fortunes commensurate with the name. The family stewards a Federal Historic Landmark (Rokeby) but is documented to lack independent operating wealth at the level required for a Phase 1 candidate.
  • Strategic verdict: Real, living, Hudson Valley-grandee-named individuals; likely below the $300M wealth threshold; should be deferred unless deeper sourcing reveals specific Aldrich descendants with independent fortunes. Notable as community-affinity prospects at modest gift tiers, but not the transformational tier. Listed as L08 with caveats.

Mortimer descendants (living)

  • The Mortimer family is historically a Tuxedo Park family (Orange County, NY) — Pierre Lorillard, Stanley G. Mortimer Jr., John Jay Mortimer. No documented Litchfield County or Dutchess County primary residence found in current research.
  • Mortimer family wealth derived from real estate investment (Richard Mortimer was in Ward McAllister's "Four 400") but has likely diffused.
  • Strategic verdict: DROP — no living Mortimer-named individual surfaced with Nuvance core service area residence at $300M+ wealth tier.

Roosevelt-DuPont line

  • The DuPont and Roosevelt families intermarried at points, but no specific living individual surfaced in this research with the combined Nuvance affinity + wealth profile. DROP absent further evidence.

Hitchcock heirs (Mellon-fortune relatives — surfaced via Hudson Valley estate, not Phase 0 list specifically)

  • The Hitchcock estate "Daheim" (Millbrook, 2,078 acres) is owned by descendants of Tommy Hitchcock Jr. via the Mellon line. Tommy Hitchcock III died in 2023; Peggy Hitchcock died April 2024. The estate was listed for $65M in 2024.
  • Strategic verdict: A succession-event-window candidate exists in principle (Mellon-tier wealth dispersed across heirs), but specific living heirs are not publicly identified. HOLD for Phase 2 deeper research.

Overall Hudson Valley grandee corridor verdict: Phase 0's prior was directionally correct but operationally constrained — the "Astor orphan" pattern is real. Most named Hudson Valley grandee descendants are below the $300M threshold. The Hitchcock/Mellon line is the most promising thread, but lacks publicly identified individuals. Robert R.
