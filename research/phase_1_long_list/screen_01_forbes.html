Phase 1 Screen 01: Forbes 400 — NY/CT residents

Compiled: 2026-05-10 Source category: Forbes 400 (2025 edition, published Sept 9, 2025) + Forbes Real-Time Billionaires Index, NY/CT residents Subagent role: 1 of 5 in Batch 1.B (parallel geographic-wealth screen) Methodology authority: research/phase_1_long_list/screening_methodology.md Phase 0 authority: sources/phase_0_verification_update.md

Context and method note

The 2025 Forbes 400 was published September 9, 2025 (44th annual edition). It now takes a $3.8B net worth to make the cut, up $500M YoY; the 400 collectively are worth a record $6.6T (source: Forbes 400 2025 Wealthiest Americans list; Facebook/Forbes 44th annual list announcement, retrieved 2026-05-10).

Scope filter applied: This screen captured Forbes 400 (and the broader Forbes Real-Time Billionaires Index where the cut is below $3.8B) individuals whose primary residence is in the Nuvance service-area-relevant counties: Dutchess, Putnam, Westchester, Rockland (NY); Fairfield, Litchfield (CT). Secondary residences flagged where known. Individuals with documented NY/CT residence histories who have since moved to Florida (Tepper, Griffin, Kovner, parts of Och) are flagged but de-prioritized.

WebFetch was denied in this session. All claims here are grounded in WebSearch result excerpts; where claims rest on a single source, the confidence label reflects that.

  • The Dyson family was ranked #41 (tied) on a Chronicle of Philanthropy historical "American philanthropists" listing (Philanthropy.com on No. 41 tied Dyson family).
  • Conservative estimate of personal/family fortune (privately held holding company + foundation + multiple operating businesses): likely in the $300M-$1B range (inferred), which means Robert Dyson meets the screen's centi-millionaire threshold ($300M+) and may meet billionaire threshold but is not Forbes-listed.

Dyson is automatically added to the Nuvance long list per Phase 0 directive (multi-decade Vassar Brothers donor, alive, active Chairman of Dyson Foundation, Hudson Valley primary residence). Notes and data gaps

Calibration findings

  1. Forbes 400 NY/CT scope is materially smaller than the Phase 0 spec anticipated. Of the 11 Forbes 400 candidates clearly in scope (plus 7 Real-Time Billionaires Index additions below the cut), only 2-3 have any documented Nuvance-area healthcare affinity beyond simple residence. The structural finding from the Phase 0 audit — that Nuvance-region centi-millionaires (not Forbes-tier billionaires) are the strategically correct segment — is further confirmed by this screen.

  2. Florida tax-residency migration is reshaping the Greenwich/Westport cohort. Tepper, Griffin, Kovner are gone. Tudor Jones is in active transition (still owns Greenwich estate but seeking non-residency tax status). Stone may have shifted to Naples FL. Och primarily Miami Beach now. Greenwich's Forbes-400 density has declined materially over the past 8-10 years — a structural shift that affects every CT-focused fundraising strategy.

  3. The "Forbes 400 NY/CT residents with documented Hudson Valley / Nuvance affinity" pool is very thin — perhaps 3-5 candidates at most, and most have stronger ties to NYP/Yale/Mt. Sinai than to Nuvance specifically. This is consistent with the Phase 0 audit verdict that the integrated Northwell flagship hospitals will rationally capture true-billionaire-tier philanthropy, leaving the Nuvance-region pathway for affinity-anchored centi-millionaires.

Data gaps

  1. The full 2025 Forbes 400 list with detailed residence data is not available via my WebSearch tooling. I extracted via search-result excerpts. WebFetch was denied. I cannot verify (e.g.) that there is not a Westchester or Putnam resident at #150-#400 that none of the patch.com / westchestermagazine / dailyvoice articles named. Recommendation: orchestrator or a downstream subagent should validate this screen against the full Forbes 400 list directly when WebFetch is enabled.

  2. Westchester Magazine's "8 Westchester Residents Make The Forbes 400 Richest List" (2023) article content is not available — only the title via WebSearch. The eight residents are likely a near-superset of the names I captured (Soros, Lauren, Peltz, Secunda, Och, Siegel, and likely Einhorn + one other) — but full verification is blocked by the WebFetch denial.

  3. Robert Dyson's personal net worth is not publicly tracked. Phase 1 personalization for Dyson must rely on Dyson Foundation financial data + DKM private-company estimates rather than a Forbes-style number.

  4. The Whittingham family deaths reframe the Phase 0 verification update materially. This finding should propagate back into the Phase 0 verification update at next revision opportunity — Section 5 of that document is wrong on Andrew & Michael Whittingham being alive.

  5. Several billionaires have ambiguous primary residence: Stone (FL/CT), Och (FL/NY/CO), Tudor Jones (FL/CT), Boehly (CT/London). These should be flagged in Phase 2 dossiering and resolved with public-records / tax-filing inquiry where the relationship economics merit.

  6. Ostrover residence (New Canaan vs. Greenwich) is unresolved across sources — likely both at different times; would benefit from current verification.
