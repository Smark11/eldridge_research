Where Bloomberg's number differed materially from Forbes, both are reported.

### Centi-millionaire wealth estimate caveat

For all centi-millionaire entries (Section "Centi-millionaires" above), the net-worth estimate is `(inferred)`. Methodology used:
- For public-company executives (Norwitt): triangulate from insider-trading filings (Benzinga, GuruFocus, CoreStreet) — each method yields different number, range given
- For ex-executives (Nooyi): triangulate from documented gifts + board-pay + likely vesting + public-co share grants
- For inherited-wealth recipients (Goldman, Gottesman): triangulate from family-trust public filings + documented gifts
- For documented major-gift donors (Schwartz): inferred lower-bound from $10M+ gift capacity + public bio + family wealth indicators

Wealth estimates for non-Forbes-listed individuals are accurate within approximately a factor of 2x.
