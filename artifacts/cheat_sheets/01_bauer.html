Cheat Sheet — George & Carol Bauer.

Rank: #1 | Tier: Centi-millionaire | Suggested ask: $50-75M+ (Yale-Divinity-precedent perpetuity-endowment legacy structure); $5-15M is the scaled-down floor if the legacy frame doesn't land Primary motivation: Repayer + Devout | Top intro path: Laura + Peter Cordeau (Norwalk Hospital president) within 60 days

What you know (in 60 seconds)

  • George (b. 1931, age 94) and Carol (b. ~1932-33, age 93) celebrated 70th wedding anniversary April 2025; longtime Wilton home (206 Dudley Rd) sold November 2024 for $2.1M — transition signal
  • George founded USAmeriBancorp (sold to Valley National Bancorp for $816M January 2018 — late-life liquidity event funding the 2019-2024 transformational gift cluster); also Bankwell Financial Group director (~$18M BWFG stock); GPB Group private investment-banking firm (founded 1987 after 31-yr IBM executive career)
  • Three children, all on Bauer Foundation board: Jocelyn, Jennifer Bauer Toll (Wilton/New Canaan/Naples FL), Brad; plus 5 grandchildren
  • $35M+ direct giving to Norwalk Hospital ($15M Patient Pavilion lead + $20M Bauer Family Pavilion expansion 2022); helped raise $50M+ more from peers
  • Carol: first woman elected Board Chair, Norwalk Hospital Trustees; certified chaplain (~2002) specializing in maternal-infant loss in the Jeffrey Peter Bauer NICU she helped name (in memory of their firstborn son who died at 5 days old in the early 1960s)

Three opening questions tailored to them

  1. (Repayer activation): "As the Pavilion gets ready to open, what does it mean to you to know the spiritual-care program Carol built will be a permanent part of the building?"
  2. (Devout activation): "Looking at your work at Yale Divinity, the Olin School ethics professorship, the Bauer Center at WashU — how do you think about what makes a gift institutionally generative for the long term?"
  3. (Family/Dynast activation): "How are Jocelyn, Jennifer, and Brad thinking about how the Foundation engages with Norwalk Hospital going forward?"

Two reference points to mention naturally

  1. Greg Smith (Maplewood Senior Living CEO, Pavilion campaign co-chair) — George's "mentee"; ideal co-host for next-gen engagement dinner
  2. Yale Divinity Living Village / Carol B. Bauer Hall — proves theological-stewardship-of-vocation framing resonates at $25M+ tier

The single motivation hypothesis to test

Hypothesis: Their largest future gift will be an endowment structure tied to vocational/spiritual-care perpetuity, NOT a current-use construction gift. The "Sunday-Monday connection" frame from their Princeton Faith & Work involvement is the right language.

What confirms: They engage with multi-generational endowment language; they reference perpetuity / legacy / chaplaincy frame; they bring up the three children proactively

What disconfirms: They redirect to specific clinical or facility names; they treat the conversation transactionally; they decline next-step engagement

If conversation goes well — the gentle next step

NOT the ask. The next-step setup: "Carol, George — at the right moment, I'd love to bring the three children in for a conversation about what continuation of the Bauer family's Norwalk Hospital relationship looks like for the next generation. Would you be open to that?"

If asked "How can I help?" early

"You've already done so much for Norwalk Hospital — Jeffrey Peter's name, the Pavilion, what Carol built in spiritual care. What I'd love to do is make sure we're in conversation about what your continuing partnership with us looks like in this next chapter. May I come back in 4-6 weeks with our president?"
