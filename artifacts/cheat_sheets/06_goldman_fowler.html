Cheat Sheet — Amy Goldman Fowler.

Rank: #6 | Tier: Billionaire ($3.0-3.3B Forbes 2025-26) | Suggested first gift: $5-25M (mid $10-15M) for Goldman/Fowler Women's Health Center at Vassar Brothers Patient Pavilion Primary motivation: Altruist + Dynast | Top intro path: First cousin Lloyd Goldman (Northwell trustee, $100M+ Northwell lifetime donor) via Northwell system CEO John D'Angelo — Goldman-cousins-Northwell conversation, then route to Vassar Brothers case

What you know (in 60 seconds)

  • Amy Goldman Fowler (b. 1954, age 71); 200-acre farm in Rhinebeck NY (Dutchess County) — directly in Vassar Brothers core service area; multi-decade primary residence
  • One of four Sol Goldman heirs (siblings Allan d. 2022, Diane, Jane). Wealth structurally illiquid (~95% in Solil Management NYC real estate portfolio); $27M+ lifetime Democratic political giving demonstrates personal-liquid capacity
  • TRANSFORMATIONAL WARM-INTRO PATH: Her first cousin Lloyd Goldman is the largest single donor in Northwell's history — $100M+ lifetime, Nov 2023 lead gift on Goldman Medical Pavilion at Lenox Hill (opening 2026), Northwell trustee, Feinstein board
  • Husband Cary Fowler = 2024 World Food Prize laureate, Svalbard Seed Vault founder (Nobel-nominated), former US Special Envoy for Food Security; cancer survivor (Repayer-relevant)
  • Vehicles: Amy P. Goldman Foundation + Lillian Goldman Charitable Trust (Jane-controlled, $105M healthcare-funding); personal foundations $26M combined assets ~$3.2M annual giving
  • Civic infrastructure: Vice Chair NY Botanical Garden; former Seed Savers Exchange chair; former Center for Jewish History Chair; published 5 books on heirloom vegetables; 2021 Garden Club of America Florens DeBevoise Medal
  • Healthcare giving so far: Modest — Englewood Hospital, Planned Parenthood of the Mid Hudson Valley
  • CRITICAL RISK: Active 2024-2026 Delaware + NY litigation pits Amy against sister Jane — Goldman-sibling cohort approach is OFF until resolved

Three opening questions tailored to them

  1. (Geographic-residence activation): "Living in Rhinebeck for as long as you have, what's your relationship been like with Vassar Brothers — for your family, for the community? We're trying to understand what 'world-class care close to home' has meant in the Dutchess context."
  2. (Cause-bridge to women's health): "Your foundation has consistently supported reproductive-rights infrastructure, and the unnamed Patient Pavilion at Vassar Brothers has a substantial women's-health component. Is that a fit you'd want to explore?"
  3. (Family/Dynast — careful given litigation): "Lloyd's work with Lenox Hill has been extraordinary. Have you and the Goldman cousins ever had a conversation about regional healthcare investment at the family level?"

Two reference points to mention naturally

  1. Lloyd Goldman + Goldman Medical Pavilion at Lenox Hill — the family precedent; she will be aware of his Northwell-level engagement
  2. Cary's cancer-survivorship + Svalbard work — connects food security to women's-health-and-survivorship — values bridge

The single motivation hypothesis to test

Hypothesis: Amy is ready to engage with healthcare giving at the regional level, but only if (a) the case for support speaks to her Altruist + Dynast values (low-recognition, heritage, place-based), (b) the relationship architecture brings her into the extended Goldman-Northwell family conversation (rather than asking her to fund a standalone cause she doesn't yet know), and (c) the sibling litigation has cooled enough that Goldman-family framing doesn't trigger her wariness.

What confirms: She engages with the cousin-cohort framing; she brings up Cary's experience proactively; she expresses interest in women's-health institutional architecture

What disconfirms: She declines Lloyd-mediated outreach; she redirects to non-healthcare causes (heritage seed, reproductive policy); she signals litigation-related family-name fatigue

If conversation goes well — the gentle next step

NOT a solicitation. "Amy — I'd love to come back to you with John D'Angelo from Northwell after the right pause. He's spent a lot of time with Lloyd over the years and has been thinking about what a broader Goldman-family Northwell-regional health conversation could look like. Would you be open to an informal lunch in a few months once that's well-framed?"

If asked "How can I help?" early

"Your perspective on what makes Vassar Brothers feel like the right place to invest — vs. flagship hospitals — would be enormously helpful as we develop a women's-health case for support. Could we come back to you with a working draft in 3-4 months?"
