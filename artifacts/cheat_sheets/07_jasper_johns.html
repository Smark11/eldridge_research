Cheat Sheet — Jasper Johns

Rank: #7 | Tier: Centi-millionaire ($300-500M est) | Suggested first gift: $1-5M (qualification-only; hard 4-month decision gate) Primary motivation: Altruist + emerging Communitarian | Top intro path: Conley Rollins (Low Road Sharon Inc. Director/Treasurer; Johns's "finance/bespoke innkeeper/local go-between")

What you know (in 60 seconds)

  • Jasper Johns (b. May 15, 1930, age 95) — among most celebrated living American artists
  • 170-acre Sharon CT estate (six contiguous parcels, six houses, converted-barn studio) since 1994; "the hermit of Sharon"; full-time resident, not a weekender
  • Low Road Foundation: $217M assets — largest private foundation in Sharon CT; $9.95M granted 2024
  • Estate plan publicly approved and LOCKED IN: Sharon P&Z (Oct 2025) + IWWC (Mar 2025) sign-off; Low Road Sharon Inc. (artist-residency entity) construction begins early 2026, pre-funded $32M as of FY2024. No family heirs. Personal estate structurally committed to the residency.
  • Successor board: Conley Rollins (Director/Treasurer), Joel Ehrenkranz (Mt Sinai VC — AVOID activating), Carlos Basualdo (Philadelphia Museum curator), Cecily Brown (artist), Julian Lethbridge (artist/family)
  • Healthcare giving: ZERO documented. ZERO documented engagement with Save Sharon Hospital advocacy despite 7 years of organizing.
  • Emerging signal: December 2025 $250K Sharon Housing Trust grant — the foundation's FIRST externally-visible Sharon civic grant in its 20-year history — late-life Communitarian broadening
  • Available philanthropic flexibility bounded by Low Road Foundation's ~$10M/year grant capacity

Three opening questions tailored to them

(NOTE: These should be directed to Conley Rollins, not Johns directly, in qualification phase)

  1. (Sharon community-pride activation): "Conley — Sharon Housing Trust was a meaningful step for the Foundation. We're wondering: is there an interest at the Foundation in the larger Sharon-community-infrastructure conversation — including the hospital that was nearly closed?"
  2. (Save Sharon Hospital civic frame): "The 5-year Northwell protection for Sharon Hospital's L&D is a remarkable community-fight outcome. We'd love to share where things stand and ask whether the Foundation has interest in the question of long-term Sharon community-health infrastructure."
  3. (Decision-rights probe, gentle): "Help us understand the Foundation's grantmaking philosophy as Jasper has continued to direct it through this stage — what makes a grant fit?"

Two reference points to mention naturally

  1. Save Sharon Hospital community-advocacy outcome — proves community pride wins; aligns with foundation's December 2025 Sharon Housing Trust direction
  2. The five Sharon residents (artists, writers) who have engaged with Sharon Hospital advocacy — community-peer framing if available

The single motivation hypothesis to test

Hypothesis: Decision-rights at Low Road Foundation at age 95 are functionally with Conley Rollins and the successor board. Johns himself may engage symbolically but not directly. The Dec 2025 Sharon Housing Trust grant signals an emerging Communitarian direction in late life. A Sharon-civic-pride pitch at the $1-5M tier is the realistic ceiling.

What confirms: Rollins engages with the Sharon-community-infrastructure framing; the board signals openness to Sharon-civic grants beyond housing; no Ehrenkranz activation occurs

What disconfirms: Rollins redirects to the artist-residency mission as the Foundation's defining work; Ehrenkranz (Mt Sinai) shapes the board's healthcare posture; no Sharon-civic appetite emerges

If conversation goes well — the gentle next step

"Conley — let me come back to you in 6-8 weeks with a one-page brief on what a Sharon Hospital community-infrastructure ask could look like, structured at the scale of the Foundation's recent Sharon Housing Trust support. No request — just a fit-test conversation. Open to that?"

If asked "How can I help?" early

"At this stage, the most generative thing you could share would be the Foundation's posture on Sharon-community-infrastructure giving going forward. We're not here to ask for anything specific today; we want to know whether the Foundation sees Sharon Hospital as part of its emerging community footprint."

Hard decision gate (4 months from first qualification contact)

If qualification reveals (a) Rollins/board are not open to Sharon Hospital-focused giving, OR (b) the Ehrenkranz Mt Sinai loyalty dominates the board's healthcare posture, then DROP this candidate and reallocate cultivation resources to the higher-affinity prospects (Bauer, Dyson, Dalio).
