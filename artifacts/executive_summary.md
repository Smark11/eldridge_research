## The strategy in one paragraph

This package identifies and develops a transformational major-gift strategy for Nuvance Health Foundation in the post-Northwell-merger (May 2026) operating context. Seven candidates have been independently surfaced and deeply researched — four centi-millionaires with deep multi-decade Nuvance affinity (Bauer, Dyson, Whittingham, Jasper Johns) and three billionaires with structural pathways into the Northwell-Nuvance system (Dalio, Rees, Goldman Fowler). The strategy is calibrated against the audited verdict that Nuvance's current case for support sustains a $75-200M cumulative range over 3-5 years from this cohort — *not* the original $500M aspiration, which the audit found institutionally premature. **The single most consequential finding:** the Bauer family's documented $35M+ cumulative giving to Norwalk Hospital, combined with the Yale-Divinity-precedent theological-stewardship framing, recalibrates the recommended Bauer ask to **$50-75M+** — materially higher than the Phase 3 dossier's initial $5-15M scoping. That single landing changes the cumulative target. The probability-weighted expected cumulative from all 7 is **~$70-110M**; the upside case (Bauer high-tier + Dalio institute-anchor + Dyson Phase II) reaches **$150-200M**.

## The most consequential findings

### Finding 1 — The Bauer escalation pathway is materially larger than scoping initially suggested

George and Carol Bauer are existing Norwalk Hospital top donors ($35M+ cumulative across multiple named units — Bauer Family Pavilion, Bauer Emergency Care Center, Jeffrey Peter Bauer NICU). George is 95; Carol is 93; the house sold in November 2024; they recently celebrated their 70th anniversary. Three children sit on the Bauer Foundation board, structuring it as an active succession vehicle.

Their philanthropic identity is anchored in two intertwined motivations: **Repayer** (their firstborn son Jeffrey Peter Bauer died at five days old in the early 1960s — the NICU is in his name; Carol later became a certified chaplain specializing in maternal-infant loss in that NICU) and **Devout** (Yale Divinity School Living Village with Carol B. Bauer Hall, Olin School ethics professorship, WashU named medical school deanship; "Sunday-Monday connection" framing).

**The Yale-Divinity-precedent reveals an ask architecture not yet applied at Norwalk:** an endowment-anchored, named-position-in-perpetuity legacy structure. At the Yale Divinity tier, they gave $25M+. Applied to Norwalk Hospital as a Bauer Family Endowment for Maternal-Infant Care + Spiritual Care, the recommended ask tier is **$50-75M+** — materially above the Phase 3 dossier's initial $5-15M scoping, which now serves only as the scaled-down floor if the legacy frame doesn't land. Cultivation window: **12-24 months given age**.

### Finding 2 — The Vassar Brothers Patient Pavilion appears unnamed; resolving naming-rights gates $100M+ asks

Nuvance's $550M, 752,000 sq ft Vassar Brothers Patient Pavilion (opened January 2021) appears to have been bond-financed without a transformational naming-gift anchor. After five years, the pavilion appears unnamed in public materials. This is the single most consequential strategic unknown of the project.

**Recommendation to Nuvance/Northwell leadership:** Resolve the pavilion naming-rights question within 60 days via internal conversation between Laura, Foundation Director, and Northwell system governance. The answer determines whether $100M+ pavilion-level naming asks (most relevant to Amy Goldman Fowler via cousin Lloyd Goldman + women's-health framing) can be architected.

### Finding 3 — The Northwell merger fundamentally reshapes the cultivation context

Nuvance is now part of an integrated 28-hospital, $22.6B-revenue Northwell system with a $1B Northwell capital commitment to Nuvance facilities over 5 years. This materially changes the case for support: donor strategy must now address (a) what gifts to Nuvance facilities buy that wouldn't be available at Northwell flagship hospitals, (b) how naming rights interact with system-level vs. facility-level governance, and (c) whether sophisticated donors will treat Nuvance and Northwell as one institution for portfolio-allocation purposes.

**Three of our 7 candidates have direct Northwell-system pathways:**
- Goldman Fowler via cousin **Lloyd Goldman** (largest Northwell donor in history — $100M+ Goldman Medical Pavilion at Lenox Hill)
- Rees via Feinstein Institutes board service (Northwell research entity)
- Dalio via potential Northwell system-level cultivation

These pathways are the strategic distinctives that emerged from this research.

### Finding 4 — The Dalio Career Academy gift was routed through CHRO, not Foundation — Laura's structural integration is urgent

Ray Dalio's June 2025 $5.1M gift to the Nuvance Career Academy at Norwalk/Danbury was routed through Nuvance CHRO Katie Cullinan as a workforce/HR initiative. Laura Eldridge and the Foundation were not named in the press release. This means an active major-donor relationship currently runs outside the Foundation's institutional ownership. Before the **June 2026 12-month milestone and the November-December 2026 18-month milestone** of the gift, Laura must integrate into the stewardship channel to position any escalation conversation. The recommended escalation pathway is a "Dalio Health Equity Institute at Nuvance" at the $25-50M tier, modeled on the existing NYP Dalio Center for Health Justice ($50M, 2021).

## Recommendations for Nuvance/Northwell leadership

These three institutional moves are conditions for the gift table to land at the high end:

1. **Resolve the Vassar Brothers Patient Pavilion naming-rights question within 60 days.** Gates $100M+ asks. (See Finding 2.)

2. **Define one signature institute (Cancer most likely, given Smilow-architecture precedent) by end FY2026 with a $25-75M founding-donor opportunity.** Most plausible anchor: Ray and Barbara Dalio. (See Finding 4.)

3. **Allocate the $1B Northwell capital commitment publicly to year-1 anchor projects.** Donors give to specific projects, not aggregate commitments. Definitional specificity is the unlock.

Three additional operational moves recommended:

4. **Institute a Luminate-equivalent annual award ceremony at Norwalk Hospital** (Greenwich Hospital precedent) — creates the recognition ladder that elevates current donors and identifies prospects.

5. **Build a long-tenured-physician honor-gift pipeline.** Many of our benchmark cases (Greenwich Smilow $25M anonymous DAF gift Dec 2025 honoring Dr. Hollister) came through grateful-patient networks attached to specific clinicians, not "billionaire cultivation."

6. **Build dual-track campaign architecture** when Nuvance next launches a capital campaign (comprehensive multi-year + standalone facility-anchor — Greenwich Arc of Care + Smilow Cancer Hospital building fund precedent).
