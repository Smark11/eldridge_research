i.2 — A candid read

The $500M
question.

The original aspiration was framed around a $500 million cumulative campaign target. The research does not support that figure as currently achievable. It supports $75-200 million from this candidate cohort over three to five years.

The path to a higher figure is sequenced, not foreclosed. It runs through three institutional moves: resolving the Vassar Brothers Pavilion naming, defining one signature institute at the $50M-founding-donor tier, and publicly allocating the $1B Northwell capital commitment to year-one anchor projects.

Three moves emerged from this research that, if executed in the next twelve to twenty-four months, would materially alter what the cohort is capable of producing.

Finding I

The Bauer ask
is materially larger
than initial
scoping suggested.

Phase 3 originally scoped the Bauer ask at $5-15M as an escalation gift. The Phase 4 Greenwich case study surfaced evidence that revises this upward to $50-75M+.

Finding I — the recalibration

$50–75M+

The recommended Bauer ask, structured as a perpetuity-endowment legacy architecture mirroring the Yale Divinity Living Village precedent.

The Bauers have given more than $25M to Yale Divinity School (Carol B. Bauer Hall); $35M+ cumulative to WashU Olin (Bauer Hall, Leadership Center, Leaders Academy); and a named medical-school deanship. Their non-Norwalk giving at the $25M-per-institution tier is the evidence that the Norwalk ask architecture has been undersized.

Finding I — three structural factors

Why this family,
not just this gift.

i.

The family-tragedy anchor.

The Jeffrey Peter Bauer NICU is named for their firstborn son, who died at five days old in the early 1960s. No other institution can replicate this; the anchor is at Norwalk.

ii.

Carol's 47-year tenure.

Carol Bauer has volunteered at Norwalk since 1978; she became a certified chaplain specializing in maternal-infant loss in the NICU she helped name. The deepest possible institutional embedment.

iii.

The theological frame.

The "Sunday-Monday connection," Marcus Borg's "Other Reality," the Princeton Faith & Work board. The ask language honors the spiritual dimension of their giving — that is what activates the upper tier.

10

Finding II

The Vassar Brothers
Patient Pavilion
appears unnamed.

A $550 million, 752,000-square-foot pavilion opened in January 2021. After five years, no naming-gift anchor appears in public materials. If this is true, it is the single most consequential unsold naming opportunity in the post-merger Nuvance system.

11

Finding II — the institutional gate

$550M

The pavilion construction cost. The naming-rights resolution gates whether $100M+ pavilion-level asks are credible — and converts the Goldman Fowler candidate from a $5-25M prospect into a $25-50M+ prospect.

Recommended action: sixty-day internal inquiry between Laura, Foundation Director, and Northwell system governance to confirm pavilion-level naming-rights status. The answer is the single most consequential institutional fact in the package.

12

Finding III

The Dalio
June 2026
milestone is now.

Dalio Education's $5.1M June 2025 gift was routed through Nuvance CHRO Katie Cullinan, not the Foundation. The twelve-month milestone — June 2026 — is the moment integration happens or the relationship calcifies in the wrong reporting line.

Finding III — the sequence

A path to $50M.

  • End May 2026 Direct outreach from Laura to Katie Cullinan and Rosa Ortiz (Dalio Education Chief of Staff). Framed as institutional stewardship coordination.
  • Mid June 2026 Twelve-month milestone impact reporting. Foundation co-attribution. Barbara Dalio invited.
  • Jul-Sep 2026 Stewardship deepening via site visit; introduction of Foundation Director and Robertson/D'Angelo. Not solicitation; relationship-build.
  • Oct-Dec 2026 Eighteen-month window. Soft introduction of institute-naming thesis (Dalio Health Equity Institute at Nuvance, or Behavioral Health Institute).
  • 2027 Formal institute-naming ask. Team: Laura + D'Angelo + Robertson + peer donor. Tier: $25-50M+. Co-branded "Dalio [Institute] at Nuvance."
