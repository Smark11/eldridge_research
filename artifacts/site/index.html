A note before

To the reader.

This catalog assembles every artifact produced for the Nuvance Portfolio research package — forty‑some files across eight phases of work, indexed and described, with the most consequential moments surfaced at the top. It is the door to everything else. The catalog itself is brief; the underlying work is dense. Read this page first; drill into whatever interests you.

The package was built over three days in early May 2026 as an independent research curiosity that turned into something worth handing to a particular person — a CFRE working a particular institution in a particular operating context. It is a gift, in the most literal sense. Nothing is asked of you in return. Use as much or as little of it as is useful. Forward it. Set it aside. The momentum was the research's, not the recipient's.

Three movements organize the work. The first establishes the strategic frame — where Nuvance sits in May 2026, what the merger with Northwell has and has not changed about the case for support, and what realistically can be raised from the candidate cohort over the next three to five years. The second introduces the seven candidates: who they are, why they were selected, what the research surfaced about each. The third distills the three findings of consequence — the moves that, if executed, would materially alter outcomes.

Every factual claim in this package is sourced — to live web research, to public filings, to retrieved articles. Where inferring rather than reporting, it says so. Where uncertain, it labels it. Where a strong recommendation diverges from what the conventional read would be, the divergence is visible and the reasoning is traced.

i

Part one

Three findings of consequence.

The moves that, if executed within twelve to twenty‑four months, would materially alter the cohort's transformational‑gift outcomes.

I the recalibration

The Bauer ask is materially larger than initial scoping suggested.

Phase 3 originally scoped the Bauer ask at $5–15M as an escalation gift. The Phase 4 Greenwich Hospital case study surfaced evidence — $25M+ to Yale Divinity, Olin School ethics professorship, named medical‑school deanship at WashU — that revises the recommended ask upward to $50–75M+, structured as a perpetuity‑endowment legacy mirroring the Yale Divinity Living Village.

$50–75M+

II the structural question

The Vassar Brothers Patient Pavilion appears unnamed.

Nuvance's $550M, 752,000 sq ft Patient Pavilion — the largest hospital construction project in Hudson Valley history at the time of its 2021 opening — appears in the research to have no lead‑donor naming attached after five years. If pavilion‑level naming is institutionally available post‑merger, this is the single structural anchor for a $100M+ ask. Resolving the question is a 60‑day internal conversation that gates substantial downstream major‑gift value.

$550M

III the channel integration

The Dalio relationship currently runs outside the Foundation's institutional ownership.

Ray Dalio's June 2025 $5.1M gift to the Nuvance Career Academy was routed through CHRO Katie Cullinan as a workforce / HR initiative; Laura Eldridge and the Foundation are not named in the press release. Before the June 2026 12‑month and December 2026 18‑month stewardship milestones, the Foundation needs to integrate into the active stewardship channel. The escalation pathway — a Dalio Health Equity Institute at Nuvance, geographically differentiated from the existing NYP Dalio Center for Health Justice — opens from that integration.

June 2026

A note at the end

Colophon.

The Nuvance Portfolio was assembled over three days in early May MMXXVI by Mark Senerth in collaboration with Claude Opus 4.7, working from public sources — Forbes filings, Form 990‑PF records, news archives, foundation annual reports, retrieved articles, regional business journals, and a great deal of triangulation.

Eight phases of work produced forty‑four catalogued artifacts: institutional context audit, a long list of fifty candidates surfaced from five parallel source streams, a short list of seven after affinity‑first selection, seven deep dossiers, eight benchmarking case studies, tactical artifacts (cheat sheets, cross‑reference matrix, warm‑intro network map), a tailored strategy playbook, an executive summary, a designed pitch deck, an executive presentation, and a 31‑page bound research monograph.

Approximately twenty‑seven parallel subagent dispatches plus extensive orchestrator‑led synthesis. Forty‑some commits on the main branch, tagged v1.0. Every factual claim in the package is source‑cited; every inference is labeled; every verification gap is documented for follow‑on resolution.
